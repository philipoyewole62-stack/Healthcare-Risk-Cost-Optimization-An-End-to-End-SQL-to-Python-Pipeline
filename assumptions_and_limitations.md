# Assumptions and Limitations

## Key Assumptions

- Length of Stay (LOS) is used as a proxy for hospitalization cost.
- Daily cost benchmarks are assumed to be constant across patients.
- Laboratory abnormality flags are simplified into binary indicators.
- Correlation analysis is used for exploration, not causal inference.

---

## Limitations

- Actual billing-level cost data is not available.
- Clinical severity beyond lab abnormalities is not explicitly modeled.
- Results may vary across hospitals with different operational structures.
- Correlation does not imply causation and should not be interpreted as predictive.

---

## Interpretation Guidance

Findings should be interpreted as **risk indicators**, not deterministic outcomes.  
The framework is intended to support decision-making, not replace clinical judgment.
